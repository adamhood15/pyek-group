Before the turkey hits the table, Bunga’s whipping up something sweet! Here’s his favorite cinnamon roll recipe to make your Thanksgiving morning extra special.

These warm, gooey cinnamon rolls are packed with sweet cinnamon flavor and topped with a frosting so good, you’ll want to lick the spoon. Ready to bake up a little deliciousness?

Let’s roll!

For the Dough

3 ½ cups all-purpose flour

2 ¼ tsp active dry yeast (1 packet)

¾ cup warm milk

¼ cup granulated sugar

¼ cup melted butter

1 egg

½ tsp salt

For Bunga’s Cinnamon Swirl

½ cup softened butter

¾ cup brown sugar

2 tbsp ground cinnamon

For the Sweet Swirl Frosting

4 oz softened cream cheese

2 tbsp softened butter

1 cup powdered sugar

½ tsp vanilla extract

Mix the warm milk, yeast, and a pinch of sugar. Let it sit for 5–10 minutes until foamy. Add the remaining sugar, melted butter, egg, salt, and flour. Knead until smooth, cover, and let rise for about an hour until doubled in size.

Roll the dough into a rectangle and spread on the softened butter. Sprinkle evenly with brown sugar and cinnamon. Now that’s what I call a sweet storm!

Roll the dough into a log and slice into 12 equal rolls. Place them in a greased baking dish, cover, and let rise for another 30 minutes.

Bake at 350°F for 20–25 minutes, until the rolls are lightly golden and cooked through. Your kitchen is about to smell AMAZING!

Beat the cream cheese, butter, powdered sugar, and vanilla until smooth. Spread that creamy goodness over your warm cinnamon rolls.
