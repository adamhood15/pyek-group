Frequently Asked Questions

When does Typhoon Texas Open for the Summer?

Typhoon Texas is now open for the 2026 Summer Season! See our Hours and Operations Calendar for more details.

What time does Typhoon Texas Open and Close?

The park typically opens up at 11:00 AM each morning. Close times are usually anywhere from 5:00 PM - 10:00 PM depending on the day. Be sure to check our Hours and Operations Calendar for the day you want to visit!

Is getting a Season Pass to Typhoon Texas worth it?

We may be a little biased but the short answer is YES! The Season Pass pays for itself in two visits. To see all the amazing perks you get with a Season Pass check out our Season Pass Perks page.

What's the best age for visiting Typhoon Texas?

Typhoon Texas Houston is designed for guests of all ages. Younger visitors can have a blast in areas like Typhoon Jr., while families can relax together floating along the Lazy-T River. For those ready for more excitement, there are high-energy slides and attractions that deliver bigger thrills. Whether you’re visiting with little ones or older kids looking for adventure, there’s something for everyone to enjoy at their own pace. See our attractions page for all the great rides we have!
