The Ride

A Surge of Pure Adrenaline

Climb to the top of our seven-story slide tower and choose your fate: plunge into the dark enclosed chute or face the open-air half-pipe with sweeping views of the park below. Either way, your heart is racing before you even push off.

Tidal Wave Tower combines steep drops, high-speed banking turns, and a dramatic splash-down finale that sends a wall of water ten feet into the air. Whether you ride solo or race a friend on the dueling lanes, this is the signature thrill at Typhoon Texas — and you'll be back for more.

Know Before You Ride

Safety & Restrictions

Minimum height 48″. Children under this height will not be permitted.
Not recommended for guests with heart, back, or neck conditions, or who are pregnant.
Life jackets available free of charge at the ride entrance for those who want them.
Loose items must be secured or stored in a free locker before boarding.
Maximum weight limit of 300 lbs. Combined weight limit applies for dueling lanes.
Guests must position themselves per ride attendant instructions before launch.
