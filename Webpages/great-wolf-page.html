WATER PARK ESSENTIALS

What to Bring

  • Comfortable swimwear and water shoes for little ones (plus sunscreen during warm-weather months)
  • No outside food or drink needed - grab delicious snacks and meals poolside

Best Time to Visit

  • Arrive early in the day for shorter lines on popular attractions

Safety

  • All guests can access life jackets for use in the water park
  • Lifeguards stationed throughout the park
  • Height restrictions clearly marked at each attraction

Pro Tips

  • Download our mobile app for food ordering, daily schedules, and more
  • Lockers are available to store your belongings

Frequently Asked Questions

See All FAQs See All FAQs
What are the water park hours at the Great Wolf Lodge in Houston, TX?

For water park hours during your stay, please view our Indoor Water Park page. You can access the water park any time after opening on your arrival day and until closing time on your departure day.

How much is a water park day pass at the Great Wolf Lodge in Houston, TX?

Wolf Lodge in Webster, TX with a Day Pass. Please see our Day Pass Page for pricing on both Half Day and Full Day Passes. Passes are free for children ages 2 and under.

What is included in a stay at Great Wolf Lodge?

Great Wolf Lodge guests have unlimited access to the water slides and pools inside the 84-degree water park. Guests are also invited to enjoy free activities like Story Time and Yoga Tails in the Lobby. There are attractions inside the Adventure Park that are available at an additional cost.

Are life jackets provided?

Coast Guard approved life jackets are available on a first come, first serve basis. We strongly recommend that children under 48 inches and weak or non-swimmers wear a life jacket. Please note that inflatable toys are not allowed in the water park.

Are towels provided in the water park?

Towels are provided for your family in the water park as well as bathing towels in your suite. You may wish to bring a beach towel or cover up as we ask that the towels stay in the water park area. Towels in the water park are included but are subject to a fee if not returned.

Are lockers available in the water park?

We do have a locker room with small lockers available for your use. The lockers are rented on a daily basis for a nominal fee. Larger items, such as luggage, will need to be kept in your room or vehicle.
